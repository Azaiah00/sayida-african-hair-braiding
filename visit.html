Before you come

Six things that make the day go smoothly.

  • Book at least 48 hours ahead

    Square takes bookings from 48 hours out to a year ahead. Need it sooner? Call (336) 337-3257.

  • Plan for your chair time

    Every style on the price list shows its time. Small box braids, for example, are about 8 h 30 min.

  • Locs, men’s and custom styles: consult first

    Book the free 30-minute consultation with Sayida and add the style you want in the appointment note.

  • Bring a reference photo

    Show the look you want. One client wrote that her hair came out just like her photo.

  • Ask about payment and hair

    When you book, ask about payment options and whether hair is included for your style.

  • Going knotless?

    Boho and twist styles can be made knotless with the +$30 add-on on Square.
