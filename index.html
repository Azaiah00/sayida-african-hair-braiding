Questions

Asked before booking

What are Sayida African Hair Braiding’s hours?

Monday to Saturday 6 AM to 10 PM, and Sunday 2 PM to 10 PM, by appointment. Those are the hours on the studio’s Square booking page.

How far ahead do I need to book?

Square takes online bookings at least 48 hours ahead and up to a year out. If you need something sooner, call or text (336) 337-3257 and ask.

How much do knotless braids cost?

On Square, knotless braids are $185 for large (about 4 h), $200 for medium (about 6 h 30 min) and $250 for small (about 8 h). The final price is confirmed when you book.

Do you do locs?

Yes: micro locs, sister locs and original locs. Each starts with a free 30-minute consultation with Sayida, where you get your quote. Book the consultation on Square.

Where is the studio?

It is a private home studio in North Greensboro at 5716 River Glen Dr, Greensboro, NC 27455. Your appointment confirmation from Square has the details.

How do I pay?

Ask about payment options when you book, so you know what to bring on the day.

Is hair included?

Ask when you book whether hair is included for the style you chose, so you know whether to bring your own.

Do you do sew-ins, tree braids or retwists?

Sayida’s Facebook page lists sew-ins, tree braids and retwists. They aren’t on the Square menu, so call (336) 337-3257 to book them.
